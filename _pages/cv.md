Research topics
======
* Model generalizability and interpretability
* Magnetic-acoustic-electrical tomography (MAET) reconstruction and conductivity imaging
* AI-assisted diagnosis and therapy for nasopharyngeal carcinoma and liver disease
* Segmentation, classification, registration, and image generation for medical images

Research projects
======
* **Assisted diagnosis and therapy for nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC)**: Developed a deep learning framework to automatically segment NPC and organs-at-risk from multi-modal images, resulting in 3 patents and a paper in IEEE TMI.
* **Ultrasound model generalization**: Improved the cross-device generalization of medical ultrasound models with manifold learning and style randomization, leading to a MICCAI 2023 paper and an IEEE JBHI paper.
* **Uncertainty in large-scale foundation models**: Developed the first Bayesian framework for the Segment Anything Model (SAM) to generate uncertainty-aware predictions (MICCAI 2025).
* **Liver disease diagnosis**: Designed deep learning models using privileged information and attention mechanisms to diagnose multiple liver diseases from multi-modality ultrasound images.
* **MAET reconstruction**: Developed the first deep learning–based reconstruction method for MAET and a physics-informed neural network incorporating Maxwell's equations for high-quality electrical conductivity reconstruction.
